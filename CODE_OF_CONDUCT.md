# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background or experience. Treat people with respect. Explain technical disagreements, accept corrections, and give feedback on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. They will consider the context and severity of each report and avoid sharing private details unnecessarily.

To report misconduct, contact a maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. If that route is unavailable or a maintainer is involved, use GitHub's Report abuse feature.
